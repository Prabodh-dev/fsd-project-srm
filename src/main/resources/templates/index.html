Everything an exam needs

Server enforced timer

The clock lives on the server. Reloading, reconnecting or changing devices restores your answers and the exact time left.

Proctoring signals

Tab switches and window changes are logged with timestamps. Exams submit automatically once the violation limit is reached.

Randomised papers

Question and option order can be shuffled per student and stays stable for the whole attempt.

Instant grading

Answers are graded the moment an exam is submitted, with a full review of correct and chosen options.

Teacher analytics

Score distribution, pass rate, completion time and per question accuracy highlight what to teach next.

Printable results

Download a PDF report or print a clean result page. Teachers can export every result as CSV.
